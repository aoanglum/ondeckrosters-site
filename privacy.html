Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

OnDeck Rosters is built to work without knowing anything about you. This page explains exactly what the app does and does not do with data.

What we collect

Nothing. OnDeck Rosters does not have user accounts, does not use analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not transmit any information that identifies you or your device to us or to any third party.

What stays on your device

Your favorite teams, your pinned "home" team per league, and your Study Mode progress are all stored locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults and SwiftData). None of it is uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, it's gone.

Roster and depth chart data

Team, player, and depth chart data shown in the app comes from public sports data sources (including ESPN, MLB, and NHL). This data is about public figures (professional athletes) in their professional capacity, not about you, and fetching it doesn't require or transmit any personal information.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or data-broker SDKs. It talks to the public sports data sources above in order to show up-to-date rosters, and to nothing else.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds an account system — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at support@ondeckrosters.app.
