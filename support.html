Support

Here to help with anything in the app, or in a roster that looks wrong.

A roster or depth chart looks out of date or wrong

Pull down on the depth chart screen to force a refresh. Rosters update automatically, but trades, call-ups, and waivers can take a little while to land — if something still looks off after a manual refresh, let us know which team and what's wrong and we'll take a look.

How do I set my favorite team?

Open the team picker (tap the team name at the top), pick your team, then tap the pin icon. Each of the four leagues remembers its own pinned team, so you can follow a different team in each.

What is Study Mode?

Study Mode turns your favorited teams' real rosters into flashcards and quizzes, with spaced repetition so the names you miss come back around more often. Find it from the brain icon on any league screen.

Does the app require an account?

No. Everything is local to your device — see the privacy policy for details.

Still need help?

Email support@ondeckrosters.app and we'll get back to you.
